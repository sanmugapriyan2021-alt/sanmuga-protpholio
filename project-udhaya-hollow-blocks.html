Udaya Hollow Blocks (UDH) Manufacturing Portal

Architected by R. S. Sanmugapriyan • Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at PSG College of Technology • Native of Palani, Tamil Nadu.

Project Purpose & Client Objectives

Udaya Hollow Blocks (UDH) is a leading manufacturer of high-strength precast concrete hollow blocks, heavy-duty sewage rings, septic tank well lids, precast compound walls, and ventilation windows operating in Singampunari & Sivagangai.

Core Objective: Civil contractors needed an instant block dimension estimator to compute required block quantities based on wall square footage, while the factory needed a Windows Billing POS and mobile tracking for multi-truck daily dispatches.
